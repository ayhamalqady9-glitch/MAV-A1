STAGE 09 / 10 · RETURN & RECHARGE

DUST OPACITY RISING // RETURN TO HANGAR

HOME BEFORE THE DUST

A dust storm is thickening the air (optical depth τ rising): less sunlight for the lander's arrays, more grit on everything. In 2018 a planet-wide storm (τ ≈ 10.8) silenced the Opportunity rover for good. MAV-A1 lands on the raised pad, the lift lowers it into the hangar and the doors seal; it charges through an induction coil, with no exposed contacts.

Design rationale

Mars's thin CO₂ atmosphere (≈0.020 kg/m³) and low speed of sound (≈240 m/s) cap how fast a rotor tip can spin. Six-blade, high-solidity rotors recover thrust within that limit.
