## Processing notes
- **PIA02820:** only the left, Tharsis-centered hemisphere is kept, with a round transparent edge. The altitude legend (−8 → +12 km) is not included; redraw it as a HUD scale.
- **PIA09929:** split into its 6 tiles (7 holes; tile 5 has two).
- **PIA09930:** split into 3 panels. The thermal panels are enlarged with nearest-neighbour so the instrument pixels stay crisp (no invented detail).
- **PIA24584 and PIA24729:** small originals, enlarged 2× (Lanczos + light sharpening). Use them only as insets or thumbnails.
- **Science panels** (PIA09929, PIA09930, PIA02820): tones are not altered; any color grading happens in the site's shader and must not change what the data shows.
- **PIA02993** (MOLA printable globe gores) was reviewed and dropped.
